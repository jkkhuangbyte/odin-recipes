Fried rice

Description

These noodles are a quick and simple meal made with vegetables, sauce, and freshly cooked noodles.

The recipe is suitable for a weekday lunch or dinner and can be adjusted using whatever vegetables are available.

Ingredients

  • 200 grams of noodles
  • One carrot
  • One bell pepper
  • Two tablespoons of soy sauce
  • One tablespoon of cooking oil

Steps

  1. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Slice the carrot and bell pepper.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  4. Cook the vegetables until slightly soft.
  5. Add the noodles and soy sauce.
  6. Mix everything together and serve.
